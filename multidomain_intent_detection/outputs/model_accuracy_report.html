Root Cause Analysis:
  • pa_field_help (30%): Queries like "What does the ignore status field do?" get classified as pa_ignore_status instead. The model interprets field names as intent keywords. Recommendation: Add more training examples with explicit "field help" / "explain field" phrasing.
  • Prescriber (50%): Confused with prescriber_info (cap_api). Both deal with prescriber data, but Prescriber means "filter claims by prescriber" while prescriber_info means "get prescriber details." Recommendation: Add Prescriber ↔ prescriber_info to CONFUSION_PAIRS.
  • pa_follow_me_logic (50%): Confused with member_transition_status — both involve plan transitions. Even LLM cannot resolve the semantic overlap without more context.
  • mail_order_info (73.3%): Confused with PharmType — both relate to pharmacy type/channel. Add to CONFUSION_PAIRS.

Structural Patterns:
  • 8 of 12 top confusion pairs are cross-domain, meaning they also cause domain misclassification. The most systematic: claim_history_search filter intents (Pharmacy, Prescriber) vs cap_api lookup intents (pharmacy_info, prescriber_info).
  • The naming convention itself causes confusion: capitalized intents (Pharmacy, Prescriber) are claim filters, while _info suffixed intents are entity lookups. The model doesn't have this naming convention as a feature.
  • Recommended new CONFUSION_PAIRS entries: mail_order_info ↔ PharmType, Prescriber ↔ prescriber_info, plan_summary ↔ member_coverage, pa_follow_me_logic ↔ member_transition_status.
